Bold and italic text you can paste anywhere

Type your text, click a style, and paste it into LinkedIn, X, Slack, Teams or email. Works in any browser on Windows, Linux or Mac.

    Shortcuts: Alt + 1 to 0 copies a style Bookmark this page: Ctrl + D In Edge or Chrome, use Install in the address bar to open it like an app.

    Style the hook

    LinkedIn shows two or three lines before "see more". Make the first line bold and leave the rest plain.

    Skip hashtags

    A styled hashtag is a different hashtag, so nobody will find it. Keep hashtags and names plain.

    Private by design

    Everything runs in your browser. Your text is never sent anywhere, and it works offline once loaded.
